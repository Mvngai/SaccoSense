Your all in one Sacco automation WebApp

SaccoSense is an intelligent, web-based Savings and Credit Cooperative (SACCO) management system built for small and medium-sized SACCOs in Kenya. It is designed to simplify how members save, borrow, and track their financial activity, while giving SACCO administrators tools to manage records, process loans, and monitor transactions more efficiently.

Beyond basic record-keeping, SaccoSense aims to support smarter financial decisions through features such as AI-assisted credit scoring, personalized financial insights, and automated detection of unusual transactions.

About SaccoSense

(Simplifying SACCO management through technology)

Our platform brings together member management, savings tracking, loan processing, guarantor management and financial reporting into one centralized system.

By reducing manual paperwork and improving access to financial information, SaccoSense aims to help SACCOs operate more efficiently, accurately and transparently.

Why SaccoSense?

Many smaller SACCOs face challenges involving manual recordkeeping, inefficient loan processing, fragmented financial information and limited access to affordable digital solutions.

SaccoSense aims to address these challenges by providing a user-friendly platform that supports everyday SACCO operations and enables better financial decision-making.

Our Mission

To empower small and medium-sized SACCOs through affordable, accessible and intelligent digital solutions that simplify financial management and improve service delivery.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital solution that enables SACCOs across Kenya to manage their operations efficiently, transparently and securely.
