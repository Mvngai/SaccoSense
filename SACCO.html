Welcome to SaccoSense

Experience Convinience

Your all in one Sacco automation WebApp

SaccoSense is an intelligent, web-based Savings and Credit Cooperative (SACCO) management system built for small and medium-sized SACCOs in Kenya. It is designed to simplify how members save, borrow, and track their financial activity, while giving SACCO administrators tools to manage records, process loans, and monitor transactions more efficiently.

Beyond basic record-keeping, SaccoSense aims to support smarter financial decisions through features such as AI-assisted credit scoring, personalized financial insights, and automated detection of unusual transactions. This dashboard gives you a quick overview of your account activity within the SACCO.

Not a Member Yet? Here's How to Join

Becoming a SaccoSense member is simple. Follow these steps to get started:

  1. Click the REGISTER link
  2. Fill in your personal details, including your national ID number and phone number.
  3. Provide next of kin information as required by SACCO regulations.
  4. Submit the form and wait for account verification by a SACCO administrator.
  5. Once approved, log in using the credentials sent to your email or phone.
